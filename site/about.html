Our Values

What guides every session

No Judgement

Nobody is graded here. Every canvas in the room looks different, and that has always been the best part of the evening.

Small Rooms

Seats are limited on purpose. A smaller room means real conversation and enough time with each guest.

Slow Evenings

Two and a half hours, one glass in hand, no rush to finish. The pace is the whole product.
