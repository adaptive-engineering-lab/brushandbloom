FAQ

Everything worth knowing

Do I need to know how to paint?+

Not at all — most guests have never picked up a brush. The session is guided step by step, and there's always someone at your shoulder if you get stuck.

What's included in the ticket?+

Your canvas, all paints and brushes, an apron, guided instruction and a welcome drink. You take your painting home the same evening.

Can I come on my own?+

Plenty of people do. Seats are arranged so solo guests end up in good company, and the evening is built around conversation.

What should I wear?+

Aprons are provided, but the paint is acrylic and it does not come out. Wear something you wouldn't mind marking.

Can I cancel or change my booking?+

Yes — free cancellation up to 48 hours before the session. Inside 48 hours, get in touch and we'll do our best to move you to another date.

Is there food?+

Light snacks are on the table, and the Bloom Package adds a sharing board. For private groups we can arrange proper catering.
